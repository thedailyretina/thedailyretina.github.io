How dates get here

Only from company releases, SEC filings, FDA, EMA and ClinicalTrials.gov. Phase 3 trials are dated by the sponsor's estimated primary completion on ClinicalTrials.gov; results usually follow some months later, and these estimates change often. Company timelines are guidance, not guarantees. Each item shows when its timing was last checked.
